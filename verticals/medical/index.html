Clinics, research labs and cGMP suites are hard to lease from a floor plan. Show tenants exactly what they will get before a single wall goes up.

See It In Action Get Started Today

Photoreal 3D spec suites let developers, operators and prospective tenants walk through a medical or lab space long before construction or renovation begins.

We model the details clinical and scientific users care about most: casework and equipment placement, clearances around fume hoods and exam tables, utility drops, and how patients, staff and samples move through the suite. Decision-makers get an accurate, interactive preview, so programming conversations start earlier and leases close with fewer surprises.

Equipment-true layouts

Fume hoods, biosafety cabinets, freezers, imaging rooms and exam casework are placed at real dimensions, so users can check fit and adjacency at a glance.

The details that drive approvals

Make accessible clearances, eyewash locations, safety zones and the separation between clinical and public areas easy to see and discuss with planners and consultants.

Workflows you can walk

Trace the path from check-in to procedure room, or from receiving dock to wet lab, and spot bottlenecks while changes are still cheap.

Questions from healthcare and lab owners

How accurate are the models?

Every model is built to the dimensions on your floor plan, and equipment is placed using typical manufacturer footprints. If something on the plan does not add up, we flag it before modeling starts.

Can you show specialized equipment like MRI rooms or fume hoods?

Yes. Our library covers common lab and clinical equipment, from biosafety cabinets and ultra-low freezers to exam tables and imaging rooms. Custom items can be modeled from spec sheets.

Do you need access to the building?

No. A floor plan and a handful of photos are usually all we need. For ground-up projects, architectural drawings work just as well.

Can we show more than one layout?

Yes. Many owners show a whitebox alongside one or two fit-out options, such as a wet lab and a dry lab, so prospective tenants can compare.

How do tenants view the tour?

Share a link or embed the tour on a listing page. It runs in any modern browser on desktop, tablet or phone, with nothing to install.
